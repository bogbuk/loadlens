## Daily Report — 2026-09-15

**Project:** LoadLens
**Author:** Bogdan Bucataru

1. Shipped a feature that picks up new matching loads the moment the load board publishes them, so alerts no longer wait for the next refresh of the page.
2. Verified that feature on a live account and confirmed it works without sending the board a single extra request, which keeps us on the safe side of their terms of use.
3. Added an indicator in the panel showing when that live feed is flowing, so a customer can tell at a glance whether alerts are arriving in real time.
4. Lost the first paying lead, who backed out after hearing that an acquaintance had received a warning from the load board for using a similar tool.
5. Concluded from that loss that how heavily our product touches the load board is a real obstacle to sales rather than a theoretical risk, and made reducing it the priority for the day.
6. Reduced how often the product refreshes the board by default from once a minute to once every three minutes, and to once every ten minutes whenever the live feed is already delivering new loads.
7. Turned full page reloads from the normal path into a rare exception, allowed at most once every fifteen minutes and only when the results have genuinely gone stale.
8. Cut the amount of result pages the product pulls in the background to a fraction of the previous volume, which was the single largest source of load on the board.
9. Added an overnight pause, on by default, since brokers barely post at night and round-the-clock activity is the most conspicuous pattern a load board can see.
10. Lowered the overall footprint of the product on the load board by well over an order of magnitude, which removes the objection that cost us the lead and protects every future customer.
11. Moved all of these decisions into a single dedicated component covered by automated tests, so the limits are visible and cannot be weakened by accident later.
12. Confirmed the new settings behave correctly in a real browser, including the overnight pause and the minimum refresh interval.
13. Released version 0.7.0 of the extension containing the live feed and the reduced footprint, ready for submission to the Chrome Web Store.
14. Applied the same limits automatically to the hosted cloud browser, so customers on the hosted option get the overnight pause and the reduced activity without any action on their side.
