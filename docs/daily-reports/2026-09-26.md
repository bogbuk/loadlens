## Daily Report — 2026-09-26

**Project:** LoadLens
**Author:** Bogdan Bucataru

1. Researched what the load board's own terms and policies say about browser extensions like ours, and found a monthly limit of 500 searches per user that its automatic refresh was likely exceeding.
2. Identified this limit as the most likely reason a customer's friend received a warning from the load board, which is why our first paying lead backed out.
3. Wrote and sent a request to the load board asking for formal written approval of the extension, or guidance on what to change to qualify.
4. Added a monthly search budget to the automatic refresh, so customers stay under the load board's limit by default and their subscription is not put at risk.
5. Let customers see how many searches they have used this month, change the limit, or switch it off at their own risk, while their own manual searches are never blocked.
6. Covered the new budget with automated tests and confirmed the settings screen works correctly.
7. Prepared a new version of the extension with the search budget and side panel connection fixes, along with release notes for customers.
8. Drafted a message to win back the lead who backed out, to be sent once the new version is live in the Chrome Web Store.
