## Daily Report — 2026-09-25

**Project:** LoadLens
**Author:** Bogdan Bucataru

5. Moved the load details card into the side panel, so contacting the broker, calling or copying the load takes one click from the score shown on the board.

7. Ran an additional automated review of the finished work and fixed the three serious problems it found, including one where the panel could lose its connection to the board after a page reload.
