LoadLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

LoadLens is a Chrome extension that works on top of the freight load boards DAT One and Truckstop that you are already signed into. It scores each load's profitability (revenue per mile after deadhead, fuel and the lane market median), checks Hours-of-Service feasibility, shows broker context, plans multi-load routes and, if you turn them on, sends you alerts in Telegram. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

1. How the extension reads load boards

  • On dat.com and truckstop.com pages, LoadLens reads the load data that the board has already loaded into your own signed-in session (the page content and the board's own responses to your searches). It does not send its own requests to DAT or Truckstop APIs and does not sign in anywhere for you.
  • If you turn on the optional auto-refresh (off by default), the extension periodically scrolls your open search tab, clicks the board's own Search button or reloads that tab, so the board itself fetches fresh results. It pauses during quiet hours you can configure.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Settings — cost per mile, target rates, Hours-of-Service hours, equipment filter, alert rules, email template, display and auto-refresh preferences, the active driver, a cached diesel price.
  • Sign-in session — if you sign in, your access and refresh tokens, email and plan.
  • Install ID — a random identifier generated on first run (see section 3).

4. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your browsing history. The extension runs only on dat.com and truckstop.com pages and talks only to our own server.
  • We do not record your clicks, keystrokes, mouse movement or scrolling.
  • We do not store IP addresses in our database. Our hosting may keep short-lived technical access logs needed to run and protect the service.
  • We do not collect health, financial, payment-card or location data.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other LoadLens users see lane rate aggregates, community broker review counts and, for route planning, summaries of loads seen by the community: markets, equipment, rate, miles, weight, broker name and MC number. They never see broker contacts, comments, your identity or your notes.
  • Data partners with a LoadLens API key may receive the same load summaries (plus rate per mile and posting age). They never receive broker contacts, comments or any information about you.
  • Telegram receives alert and password-reset messages we send to your chat, only after you link it. Alerts can include the broker's name, MC number, phone, email and the posting's comments, so you can contact the broker right away. Telegram's own privacy policy applies to those messages.
  • Our hosting provider (Hetzner, Germany) stores our servers and data on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

  • You can permanently delete your account in the extension: open the LoadLens side panel, go to the Settings tab and use Delete account. This immediately removes your account, driver profiles, device records, Telegram link and alert history, and your cloud browser together with its stored profile.
  • Load records and broker reviews are not linked to your account and stay in the community data after you delete your account. We currently keep load records without a fixed expiry.
  • If you turn the cloud browser off, it is stopped and its profile is deleted after 30 days.
  • Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its storage.
  • You can also email us at the address below to request deletion.

7. Security

All communication between the extension and our server uses HTTPS. Passwords and password-reset codes are stored only as hashes. Sign-in uses short-lived access tokens with refresh rotation. The cloud browser screen is protected by a random password that changes every time you turn the cloud browser on.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and reflect the change in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
